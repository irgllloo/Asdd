Digital Arrest As Financial Crime

Rusty, Independent Researcher, India

India’s growing digital arrest fraud is emerging as a hybrid form of cyber-enabled financial crime where scammers impersonate law enforcement agencies and pressure victims into transferring money under the guise of investigation or legal action.

Digital arrest scams have rapidly expanded across India through messaging platforms, voice calls, and video conferencing applications. Fraudsters often pose as police officers, cybercrime officials, or government authorities to psychologically manipulate victims.

Digital arrest fraud represents not only a cybercrime challenge but also a broader financial enforcement issue. Public awareness and stronger cyber policing are essential to reduce the scale of such crimes.
